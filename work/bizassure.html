01Context

BizAssure is the business insurance arm of OneAssure, and it’s licensed by IRDAI.

The portal is the tool BizAssure will provide to its partner companies, so their HR and finance teams can manage the insurance themselves instead of emailing about it.

The product has three sides. This case study covers the Employer Side.

Three sides
  • Employee Side
  • Employer Side
  • BizAssure (Broker)
02Why the employer side first

Fix the source first.

  • 1Every change to the policy starts here
  • 2HR uses it weekly, employees twice a year
  • 3The broker had tools. Clients had nothing.
  • 4The costliest mistake starts here too
03How it worked before

Nobody tells HR when it’s done.

The insurance company adds them to the policy, which takes 3 to 5 working days.

Nobody tells HR when that’s done. So HR assumes the employee was covered from day one.

  1. 01HR adds an employeeEmployee not covered yet
  2. 02Broker checksEmployee not covered yet
  3. 03Insurer updatesEmployee not covered yet
  4. 04CoveredEmployee covered
04Problem statement

Employers were working blind.

  1. 1Group insurance runs all year and changes every week.
  2. 2Can’t track what they already sent.
  3. 3Hear about problems from the employee, last.
05What we heard

Five questions nobody could answer.

  • “Is my new joiner insured?”
  • “Is this employee list even right?”
  • “Where is my claim?”
  • “Why am I hearing this from the employee?”
  • “What am I paying for?”
06Who we designed for

Two people, one company, different jobs.

AnitaHR Manager

Handles insurance for ~600 employees alongside payroll and hiring.

Wants
To know what needs attention today.
Worries
A rejected claim she could have prevented.
RaviFinance Manager

Approves the spend, has never spoken to the insurer.

Wants
Numbers he can defend.
Worries
Signing a renewal he can’t explain.
